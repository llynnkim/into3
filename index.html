Products & service

What into3.studio offers

3D photobooth session

On-site. Shoot at an into3.studio booth installed in exhibitions, stores, and events. Your capture is rebuilt in 3D, ready to view in 360° from the link you receive on site.

3D avatar figurine

Online order · delivery. We craft your reconstructed 3D model into a real figurine and ship it to you. Order directly from the link you received after your booth session.

Booth partnership

Business · institutions. For businesses that want to install and run an into3.studio booth in their space. Setup and operations are arranged individually.
